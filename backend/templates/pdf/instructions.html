Before You Begin
Setup
1
Gather Your Players

This mystery is designed for {{playerCount}} players. One person should act as the Game Master and read from the Game Master Guide.

2
Distribute Character Cards

Give each player their suspect card. Study your character's background, motive and alibi carefully — you may need to defend yourself.

3
Read the Story Aloud

Have the Game Master read the Mystery Story introduction. This sets the scene and introduces the crime.

4
Begin the Investigation

Players take turns examining clues, questioning suspects, and working through puzzles. Every clue matters.

The Rules of the Game
Game Rules
  • Stay in character. You are your suspect — defend yourself, but stay truthful to what your character would know.
  • Share clues openly. Discussion and cooperation are the fastest path to the truth.
  • No peeking at the Solution Guide. Only the Game Master may read it, and only after the investigation concludes.
  • Ask the Game Master for hints if you get stuck on a puzzle — but each hint costs you.
  • Time matters. Aim to solve the case within {{duration}} minutes.

Victory Conditions
How to Win

Accuse the correct suspect, state their motive, and explain how they committed the crime. Cross-reference the clues, the timeline, and each suspect's alibi. The Game Master will confirm whether you identified the culprit.
